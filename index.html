What is Conway's Game of Life?

Conway's game of life was a game made by the mathematician John Horton Conway. Conway's game of life is a cellular automaton used to simulate simple forms of evolution.

Any live cell with less than 2 live neighbors dies

Any live cell with over 3 live neighbors dies

Any dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbors becomes alive
